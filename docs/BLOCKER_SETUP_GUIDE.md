# Blocking Online Casinos: A Setup Guide

A step-by-step guide to putting real distance between you and online gambling. It mirrors the **Protect** tab in the NoBet app, so you can work through it on your phone or keep this page open on a computer.

> **No single blocker is perfect.** New casino domains appear every day, bank blocks rely on merchant codes, and national registers do not cover offshore sites. The goal is to stack several layers, so when one fails during a strong urge the next one holds.

## 1. Quick-start checklist

If you only have 20 minutes today, do these in order:

- [ ] Delete every gambling app on your phone ([section 3](#3-iphone-remove-and-lock-out-gambling-apps))
- [ ] Turn on your bank's gambling block ([section 8](#8-turn-on-your-banks-gambling-block))
- [ ] Install BetBlocker (free) or Gamban on your phone ([section 5](#5-install-a-dedicated-gambling-blocker))
- [ ] Ask someone to set a Screen Time passcode you don't know ([section 4](#4-hand-the-screen-time-passcode-to-someone-else))

Then come back for the rest when you have more time.

## 2. iPhone: Screen Time web filter

iOS has no single "gambling" switch, but its built-in web filter can block the sites you used most.

1. Open **Settings > Screen Time**. If it is off, tap **Turn On Screen Time** and choose **This is My iPhone**.
2. Tap **Content & Privacy Restrictions** and switch it on.
3. Tap **App Store, Media, Web & Games** (called **Content Restrictions** on older iOS versions), then **Web Content**.
4. Choose **Limit Adult Websites**. This unlocks the **Never Allow** list.
5. Under **Never Allow**, tap **Add Website** and enter every casino, bookmaker, and poker site you have used. Add both the main address and the mobile one (for example `m.site.com`).
6. Open one of the sites in Safari to confirm it is blocked.

> Menu names move slightly between iOS versions. If you can't find a screen, search for "Web Content" in the Settings search bar.

Official reference: [Apple Support: Content & Privacy Restrictions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121)

## 3. iPhone: remove and lock out gambling apps

1. Delete every casino, betting, poker, and "social casino" app from your Home Screen **and** from the App Library.
2. Go to **Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > App Store, Media, Web & Games > Apps**.
3. Pick a lower age rating (for example **13+** or **12+**). Real-money gambling apps carry the top age rating, so they disappear and cannot be opened.
4. Optional: in **iTunes & App Store Purchases** (**App Store Purchases** on newer iOS), set **Installing Apps** to **Don't Allow** for the first few weeks.

A lower age rating also hides other adult-rated apps. That small inconvenience is the friction that protects you.

## 4. Hand the Screen Time passcode to someone else

Blocks you can switch off in ten seconds will not hold during a strong urge.

1. Ask someone you trust to sit with you for two minutes.
2. Go to **Settings > Screen Time** and tap **Lock Screen Time Settings** (or **Use Screen Time Passcode**).
3. Let them type a four-digit code that you do not see, and let them keep it.
4. If iOS asks about passcode recovery, let them decide. If you can reset the passcode yourself, the lock doesn't protect you.
5. Tell them what the passcode protects, and that you may ask for it during an urge. Agree in advance that the answer during an urge is "not now".

Pick someone steady rather than someone who will feel guilty saying no.

Blocker apps maintain lists of tens of thousands of gambling sites and apps, so you don't have to.

1. Install your chosen blocker from the App Store and create an account.
2. Pick the **longest block you can commit to**.
3. Allow the VPN or content-filter permission when iOS asks. That is how the block is enforced.
4. Install it on **every** phone, tablet, and computer you own, not just your iPhone.
5. Test it: open a casino site you used to visit and confirm it is blocked.

Combine this with [section 4](#4-hand-the-screen-time-passcode-to-someone-else) so the blocker is hard to uninstall on impulse.

## 6. Add a DNS filter (phone and home Wi-Fi)

A DNS filter blocks gambling domains for every app, not just the browser, and can cover your whole home network.

1. Create a free account at [nextdns.io](https://nextdns.io).
2. In your configuration, open **Parental Control > Categories** and add **Gambling**.
3. Optional: in the same tab, turn on blocking of bypass methods (VPNs, proxies, alternative DNS).
4. **iPhone:** open [apple.nextdns.io](https://apple.nextdns.io) in Safari, enter your configuration ID, and download the profile. Then go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**, tap the downloaded profile, and install it.
5. **Home router:** follow the router setup instructions in your NextDNS dashboard so every device on your Wi-Fi is covered.
6. Visit a casino site to confirm it no longer loads.

DNS category lists are not perfect. Use this together with a blocker app, not instead of one.

## 7. Other devices: Android, Mac, Windows

- **Android:** install BetBlocker or Gamban from Google Play. For a DNS filter, go to **Settings > Network & internet > Private DNS** and enter your NextDNS hostname (shown in your NextDNS dashboard, in the form `xxxxxx.dns.nextdns.io`). Delete gambling apps and remove saved cards from Google Pay merchants you used for gambling.
- **Mac:** install BetBlocker or Gamban. Then turn on **System Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy** and add the same Never Allow websites as on your iPhone.
- **Windows:** install BetBlocker or Gamban with an administrator account, and use a separate, non-administrator account day to day so the blocker is harder to remove.

## 8. Turn on your bank's gambling block

Many banks can decline gambling card payments, even on sites your blockers miss.

1. Open your banking app and search its settings for **"gambling"**.
2. **Revolut:** **Profile > Security > Payments > Gambling block**. Turning it off later takes **48 hours**, which gives an urge time to pass. ([Revolut help](https://help.revolut.com/help/profile-and-plan/security-and-personal-data/gambling-block/what-is-gambling-block/))
3. **Monzo, Starling, and many other UK banks** offer a similar block inside the app.
4. If your bank has no block, call them and ask. Many can add one on request.
5. Remove saved cards from betting accounts, browsers, and any Apple Pay or Google Pay merchants you used for gambling.

Card blocks rely on merchant codes, so some operators slip through. Do this step alongside your blockers, not instead of them.

## 9. Join a national self-exclusion register

Once you are registered, licensed operators must refuse your bets and stop marketing to you. Choose the longest exclusion period you can commit to.

### United Kingdom

- Register free with [GAMSTOP](https://www.gamstop.co.uk). It covers gambling websites and apps licensed in Great Britain.
- If you bet in person, ask about the separate betting-shop self-exclusion scheme too.

### Bulgaria

- The **National Revenue Agency** ([NRA / НАП](https://nra.bg)) keeps the national register of excluded persons. Registration is free and confidential.
- Apply in person at an NRA office, or by email signed with a qualified electronic signature.
- The minimum exclusion period is **one year**. Licensed operators must block you and report attempts to gamble.

### United States

- Self-exclusion is run **state by state**. Search for your state gaming commission's self-exclusion program.
- Many states keep separate lists for online betting and for land-based casinos. Join both if you can.
- The [NCPG help page](https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/) lists resources by state.

### Other EU countries

Most EU countries run a national register, for example:

| Country | Register | Regulator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sweden | Spelpaus | [spelpaus.se](https://www.spelpaus.se) |
| Netherlands | CRUKS | [kansspelautoriteit.nl](https://kansspelautoriteit.nl) |
| Denmark | ROFUS | [spillemyndigheden.dk/rofus](https://www.spillemyndigheden.dk/rofus) |
| Germany | OASIS | [gluecksspiel-behoerde.de](https://www.gluecksspiel-behoerde.de) |
| Spain | RGIAJ | [ordenacionjuego.es](https://www.ordenacionjuego.es) |

Elsewhere, search your country's gambling regulator for "self-exclusion". If no register exists, self-exclude with each operator directly ([section 10](#10-close-accounts-and-cut-the-marketing)).

> Offshore and unlicensed casinos ignore national registers. That is why the blocker, DNS, and bank layers matter too.

## 10. Close accounts and cut the marketing

Bonuses, free spins, and "we miss you" texts are built to pull you back.

1. Log in to each casino or betting account, open **Responsible Gambling**, and choose self-exclusion or account closure for the longest period offered.
2. Withdraw any remaining balance first, then close the account.
3. Unsubscribe from every gambling email, and reply **STOP** to marketing texts.
4. On iPhone, turn on **Settings > Apps > Messages > Filter Unknown Senders** (**Settings > Messages** on older iOS).
5. Unfollow tipsters, betting brands, and casino streamers on social media.
6. In [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com), choose to see fewer gambling ads. Check your other social apps for similar ad settings.

Do this in one sitting with your trusted person nearby. It is easier when you are not alone with the login screens.

## 11. Choose your trusted person

1. Pick someone who knows about your recovery, or who you are ready to tell.
2. Ask them directly: *"Can I text you when I have an urge? You don't need to fix it, just reply."*
3. Add their name and number in **NoBet > Settings > Trusted person**. They appear in the **Reach Out** tool during an urge.
4. Optional: share this guide with them, so they know what you have put in place.

## 12. Keep the layers working

- **Once a month**, open a few old casino sites to check they are still blocked, and add any new ones to the Never Allow list.
- **Before your exclusion or blocker expires**, renew it. Put a reminder in your calendar a week ahead.
- **After a new phone or computer**, reinstall your blocker and DNS profile before anything else.
- **If you slip**, log it honestly in the NoBet journal, then look at which layer failed and add one more. A slip is information about where the gap is, not proof that recovery doesn't work.
